Inputs are under `build/experiments/phase4d7-20261023/`; logs and raw evidence
are under `results/phase4d7-20261023/`.

`ProbCut` tests at most three ordinary captures at eligible interior scout
nodes, with SEE >=0, a quiescence screen and a legal-move search at child
depth `depth - 4`. Both must reach beta plus 200 internal score units outside
special bands. Check, history, material and draw-clock guards limit exposure.
The reduced legal search includes quiet defenses. The isolated probe mode
cannot consume/store TT bounds, recurse into pruning/LMR or learn ordering
state, but preserves genuine history and draw rules. A cutoff returns only
the original beta, without a probe PV or direct original-depth cache entry.
These rules are heuristic safeguards, not a proof of preserved strength.

### Sleep interruption and interpretation

The full attempt is **inconclusive for equal-time tactical qualification**.
UTC timestamps span 127m18.244s, while the supervisor records 75m04.849s from
its monotonic clock: a difference of 52m13.395s. Python identifies that clock
as `mach_absolute_time()`. The saved OS power log independently confirms
13 sleep intervals within the run: 59 seconds during the parent and 3,100
seconds during the candidate, totaling 52m39s. This is close to, but not exactly,
the clock discrepancy; the remaining 25.605 seconds is not causally attributed.
The candidate includes prolonged idle/maintenance sleep and a clamshell sleep.

The runner saves final scores and moves, not per-position wall/monotonic
intervals, depths, nodes or full PVs. Consequently, neither the affected
positions nor equal actual search opportunity can be certified retrospectively.
Do not label this an uninterrupted 60-second qualification, count matching
moves as a solve rate, or use it as proof of unchanged tactical strength.
The supplied mate annotations and reported mate are not independent mate proofs.

Original state remains in `puzzles-full-status.json`, outputs in
`puzzles-full-parent.txt` and `puzzles-full-on.txt`, and supervisor output in
`puzzles-full-console.log`. The separate `puzzles-full-audit.json` records
`completed_outputs_valid_timing_qualification_inconclusive`; filtered OS evidence
is in `puzzles-power-events.txt`. The supervisor's original pending-audit status
is intentionally preserved. The tool session finished; no test is still running.

### Decision and next work

Close this finite attempt with **ProbCut parked off**. The earlier bounded
screen remains useful development evidence, but its weak reused-cache timing,
no additional reported mate here, and interrupted full comparison do not
justify default promotion or a strength claim. No retuning, automatic repeat,
long match or schedule follows from this result. Before any later qualification,
freeze a new sleep-aware launch procedure that keeps the host awake and detects
suspension, then obtain authorization for that run. Preserve these original
outputs rather than replacing them with a favorable repeat.

The owner reports Sic and Gargantua find some M7/M8/M9 mates using ten threads.
Those are user-reported results under different resources, not a controlled
comparison with this one-thread audit. A future practical SMP puzzle assessment
must match thread count, positions, limits and settings; no such run occurred
here. Do not change the owner's editable runner to impose these experiment settings.

The next broader work area is **Phase 4E: runtime/platform validation and
reproducible network tooling**. ProbCut qualification can be considered separately;
it does not block that independent work or justify extending this phase through
repeated tuning. Original v0.1 acceptance gates remain open.
