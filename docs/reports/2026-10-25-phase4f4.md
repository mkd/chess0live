## Evidence checks and limits

The runner caps the complete screen at 300 seconds and each search at five
seconds. The hang watchdog is separate from the 250-ms receipt tolerance above
requested movetime. Both wall and monotonic clocks are checked during queue
waits; gaps or discrepancies above five seconds fail without replacement.
Resource allowance is rechecked throughout. Existing output paths are rejected,
and failures retain raw transcripts and partial reports.

The independent audit passed **989 iteration records and 5,776 legal PV plies**,
plus every bestmove/ponder, explicit option set and complete matrix cell.
It verified 120 distinct recorded PIDs, 120 zero exit codes, all worker totals
and leader iteration accounting, optional branch counters and 480 monitoring
observations. Receipts ranged from 0.980179 to 0.982885 seconds; the 20-ms move
overhead explains completion just below one second. Maximum observed clock gap
was 0.255062 seconds and wall/monotonic discrepancy was 3.751 ms. No illegal
output, operational failure or detected suspension invalidated the screen.

Raw timestamps are main-loop send/queue-receipt observations, not reader-thread
arrival times. PID/exit information is recorded by the harness, not independent
OS process telemetry. Diagnostics add work, short samples vary, and two
repetitions on exposed roots do not justify a general speed or strength claim.

All **37 recorded binary, network, corpus, tool and source identities** matched
the runner's before/after hashes. Independent verification recorded a later
Makefile change: its exact baseline bytes were recovered and matched both
original pins, then retained as `baseline-Makefile` and in
`baseline-audit-inputs/Makefile`. The other 36 assets were independently checked
and source inputs snapshotted before candidate edits. This post-run recovery is
explicit; the current Makefile is not presented as the screen's original input.

## Selected implementation and correctness

The only engine-source change is in `src/exchange.cpp`. Its SEE-local slider
attack lookup replaces square-by-square ray walks with a constexpr 4-KiB table
of eight ray masks per square. Occupancy still chooses the nearest blocker of
either color or any piece type; the piece-type filter is applied afterward.
Increasing-square rays use the lowest occupied bit and decreasing rays the
highest. Empty-ray guards prevent invalid bit-scan results or shifts. Pawn,
knight, king, pin, recapture and promotion rules are unchanged.

This is a candidate to reduce implementation work in existing SEE calls, not a
new pruning rule. It preserves the intended attack-mask semantics and exposes
no new UCI option. No measured speed improvement, default-policy gain or fix for
the Phase 4F.3 mate-report deficit is claimed.

Available validation:

- All 25 synthetic profiler checks passed, including SMP leader/aggregate
  distinctions, legal replies, policy counters, timing, input drift and cleanup.
- The native core/NNUE/search/policy/time suite passed, plus direct ProbCut,
  quiescence-SEE, null-move and deep-reduction checks and 43 SMP lifecycle cases.
- The new independent square-walking attack reference passed **145,024
  comparisons**, covering both colors, all target squares, edges, mixed
  occupancies, blockers, x-rays and special-move occupancies. Existing exchange
  tests passed 4,117 generated comparisons; ordering parity passed 2,264.
- Focused ASan/UBSan exchange, new attack-mask, search, ordering, quiescence-SEE
  and ProbCut checks passed. This was not a full sanitizer or foreign-runtime
  requalification. No new TSan or cross-platform execution claim follows.

The deterministic comparison completed 120 searches: all **60 parent/candidate
pairs matched exactly** at depth 6, with one worker, both default/all-on packages
and cold/warm tables across the fifteen roots. Independent raw replay passed
720 completed iterations, 3,108 legal PV plies and 240 bestmove/ponder plies.
It reconstructed all 500 recorded commands, 1,236 consumed output lines and
180 clean readiness barriers; 27 before/after/current asset hashes matched.
Both recorded process exits were zero. There were no protocol errors, bound
scores, unexpected metadata or tablebase hits. Partial-failure retention was
reviewed statically, without an injected-failure run of this local comparison.
This bounded agreement does not prove every possible SEE or search path
equivalent, nor exact multithreaded scheduling behavior.

Performance qualification is **pending**: the owner was told LM Studio could
