# Phase 4F.7 — move-hint tactical qualification

Date: October 25, 2026. Engine source: `a1b2331`.
The [predeclared protocol](../experiments/phase4f7.md) isolates TT Move Hints
from every other setting. Preparation, search-free readiness and the complete
short smoke passed, including independent raw audits. The full 60-second
comparison and a later strength campaign have not started. Playing defaults
remain unchanged; **Phase 4F.7 qualification is still pending**.

## Scope and tooling

Both roles use the same frozen Phase 4F.6 executable. `hints-off` sets
TT Move Hints=false and `hints-on` sets it to true. Both request eight threads,
Hash 64, the pinned network, LMR=true, classic timing and 20-ms overhead.
Other experimental policies, diagnostics, pondering and tablebases are off.
The original 39-position corpus and editable owner batch file are unchanged.
Only one engine is resident/searching at a time. Reserve at least 2 GiB.

The new runner reuses the existing corpus/referee logic while retaining process
IDs, complete output through EOF, zero exit status and readiness barriers. A
separately authored auditor reconstructs commands, legal PV/best/ponder moves,
scores, clocks, resources and lifecycle evidence directly from raw logs, without
the runner's result validators or summaries. Prepared pins prevent a later full
run from silently using different tools or inputs. Failed or partial attempts
remain visible; existing output directories cannot be reused.

No engine, network, threshold or default changes were made. The frozen binary
is a byte copy of the prior tested build, not a rebuild. Historical combined
policy tools, its parked campaign, normal builds and stable GUI bundle
`0.1-dev-4b8b35d` are preserved. This phase makes no new platform/runtime claim.

## Completed short smoke

The smoke ran **14:59:58–15:01:37 Europe/Helsinki**, 99.26 seconds including
setup, on October 25. All **39 positions / 78 searches** completed at 1,000 ms
per search, with the requested eight workers and alternating role order.
The independent audit passed 40 asset identities, 78 distinct recorded PIDs
and zero exits, 431 iterations, 2,375 legal PV plies, 156 legal best/ponder
plies, 2,106 commands, 2,693 consumed lines, 234 readiness barriers and 313
resource observations. All readers joined and each session recorded one EOF.
No illegal output, protocol failure, receipt overrun, changed input or detected
suspension occurred.

Both roles chose the same final move on all 39 positions and neither reported
mate within one second. Scores and completed nominal depths differed at three
positions; all other final scores/depths agreed. All three differences remain:

| Position | Hints off: score / depth | Hints on: score / depth | Best move, both |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | +10.10 / 6 | +8.84 / 5 | `f5f6` |
| 15 | +7.99 / 7 | +9.66 / 6 | `e7d7` |
| 35 | +8.81 / 6 | +7.95 / 5 | `h3b3` |

Go-to-bestmove receipts ranged from 0.980323 to 0.980668 seconds; the 20-ms
overhead explains the early stop. Maximum reconstructed monitoring gap was
0.832775 seconds and wall/monotonic discrepancy 0.679 ms. These validate the
operational limits, not speed gains. This is a single short, nondeterministic
SMP sample, with no quiet-host attestation. Different centipawn scores do not
rank mate-finding ability or playing strength. The smoke is not a substitute
for, or part of, the separate 60-second comparison.

## Reproduction and remaining steps

The commands below use the prepared local frozen binary and manifest under
`build/experiments/phase4f7/`, pinned by `tests/data/phase4f7-pins.json`.
They are checkpoint qualification commands, not a promise that a different
compiler/OS will reproduce the same native executable digest. Optional Python
dependencies prepare automatically. Each output path must be new.

```sh
make test-phase4f7
python3 tools/run.py tools/check_phase4f7_puzzles.py --mode check --output results/my-hints-readiness
python3 tools/run.py tools/check_phase4f7_puzzles.py --mode smoke --output results/my-hints-smoke
python3 tools/run.py tools/audit_phase4f7_puzzles.py results/my-hints-smoke/report.json --output results/my-hints-smoke/independent-audit.json
```

The separate full comparison needs a newly agreed quiet window: pause LM Studio
inference and other heavy work, retain AC power and Automatic mode. Its 39
positions × two configurations × 60 seconds take about 78 minutes plus setup.
During the permitted overnight window, after checking sufficient time remains:

```sh
python3 tools/run.py tools/check_phase4f7_puzzles.py --mode full --quiet-host --output results/my-hints-full
python3 tools/run.py tools/audit_phase4f7_puzzles.py results/my-hints-full/report.json --output results/my-hints-full/independent-audit.json
```

Add `--allow-daytime` only after fresh explicit owner authorization for that
particular full run. Flags record operator assertions, not verified approval or
continuous host telemetry. On macOS a foreground `caffeinate -i` wrapper can
prevent idle sleep; resource, clock and cutoff monitoring remain active. No
automatic schedule is created and this does not authorize a later match.

Review every tactical result before a fresh paired strength campaign. Supplied
mate distances, reported mates and legal PVs ending in checkmate are separate
claims; none alone proves forced mate against all defenses or playing strength.
Higher centipawn scores and nominal depths are not strength scores. Keep hints
off pending the separate strength/default review. The older all-on campaign
remains parked; broader platform/training/release work remains open.
