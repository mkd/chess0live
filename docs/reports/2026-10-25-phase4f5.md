## Implementation

The ordinary alpha-beta loop previously allocated a separate `searched_quiets`
vector, reserved enough space for every legal move, and appended prior quiet
moves. Only an ordinary quiet beta cutoff consumed it. The ordered local move
list already contains those same prior moves in the same order.

`learn_quiet_cutoff` now filters the completed prefix directly. It preserves
speculative-mode guards, countermove and killer updates, the winner's history
reward, then prior quiet penalties in their former sequence. Captures and all
promotions remain excluded; castling and double pushes retain their previous
classification. History is read after child searches/re-searches, not snapshotted
before them. No per-node replacement container, fixed move limit or search
option is added. This change applies to default search as well as optional
configurations, but fewer allocations alone do not establish measured speed.

The invariant matters for future pruning: every earlier move in the loop must
actually have been searched. If a later change adds move-skipping pruning, it
must track the searched subset explicitly. Descendant ordering scratch cannot
serve as this node's prefix, since descendants overwrite it.
