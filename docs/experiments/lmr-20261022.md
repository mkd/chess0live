19/20-ply full histories, 128 roots for each side to move. The 64 project stems
cover e4, d4, c4 and Nf3; each shard has 32 roots per side and the same family
counts. Selection seed **20261022422**, domain
`chess0live.lmr.20261022.openings.v1`. No engine evaluation, outcome filtering

The generator rejects terminal, duplicate and recorded exposed roots, including
all available saved legal game histories, previous public suites, valid test

provenance. Compact public exclusions reproduce the selected roots; local

synthetic input records are accounted for explicitly, not silently considered

nodes and unrecorded manual positions cannot be exhaustively excluded. These
limitations remain attached to any statistical interpretation.

## Acceptance and decision

The independent auditor must verify all 512 games/256 pairs, complete raw
membership, legal history/PV/bestmove/ponder replay, actual options, PGNs,
transmitted and charged clocks/increments, source/binary/network pins, process
cleanup and the aggregate resource journal. No favorable subset is accepted.
**Any clock forfeit in either role blocks eligibility for default review.**
This is stricter than the earlier candidate-only flagfall gate. Preserve both
roles' forfeit counts and scores even when this gate fails.

For a complete valid sample, each reversed-color pair contributes candidate
points divided by two, in [0,1]. Use the same conditional two-sided 95%
Hoeffding bound: `sqrt(log(40)/(2*256)) = 0.0848813447`, about **8.488 percentage
points**. This assumes independence between pairs, which related stems/shared
host do not establish. Family and shard summaries are descriptive only.

- With all operational gates passed, lower bound strictly above 50% and
  observed score at least 50.72% makes LMR **eligible for separate default
  review**: at least **299.5/512 points**. This never edits defaults automatically.
- Upper bound below 50% means **negative evidence**: at most **212.5 points**.
- Otherwise record **inconclusive**. Corrupt/incomplete evidence cannot qualify;
  a forfeit with otherwise complete valid evidence is an operational regression.

The finite sample can detect a large observed effect; it does not resolve a
small +5 Elo gain or prove absence of strength loss on every position. No
absolute rating follows. Earlier favorable but clock-failing results remain
disclosed and are never pooled or retrospectively repaired. An eligible result
applies only to this single-thread blitz condition; external opponents, longer
time controls and SMP playing strength remain separate evidence.
