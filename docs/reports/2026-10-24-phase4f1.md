# Phase 4F.1 — quiescence efficiency

Date: October 24, 2026. Parent: `b565dd440c7d448dabc904c8e97a7f192ff47cd8`.
Decision: accept the result-preserving optimization after native correctness,
scalar, sanitizer and exact-search comparisons. No new pruning or strength
promotion follows. Phase 4F returns to search work after the local build/network
tooling checkpoints.

## Change and invariants

Previously, each non-check quiescence node generated a complete legal move list,
then discarded quiet moves. Each discarded quiet move had already paid for
make/unmake and king-safety checking. `Position::legal_tactical_moves()` now
retains all legal captures and all four promotion choices, in the established
generator's order. It separately reports whether **any** legal move exists.

Once a legal witness is found, subsequent quiet moves need no legality check.
The loop still checks every subsequent capture/promotion; it does not stop after
finding a quiet witness. An empty tactical list therefore remains distinct from
stalemate. In-check quiescence keeps the full legal generator, including quiet
evasions. Terminal detection still precedes draw/stand-pat handling, preserving
checkmate priority over the fifty-move rule. Canonical make/unmake restores the
board, bitboards, counters, en-passant state, castling rights and hash.

Pseudo-legal generation itself is unchanged; this is not a dedicated staged
capture generator. Search nodes, move ordering, evaluation, LMR, time management
and all exposed options are unchanged. The optimization is active without a
new UCI switch because it preserves the existing search definition.

## Current-default comparison

`tools/profile_search_baseline.py` supplies a small, reusable comparison for
result-preserving changes. It uses all 15 existing exposed development roots
(three openings, six middlegames, six endgames), depth 6 and two repetitions.
Each role searches cold and then immediately warm; role order alternates AB/BA.
There is one active search worker and two resident engines. Readiness is outside
the measured interval. The complete run finished in 30.734 seconds, within the
fixed 240-second overall limit and ten-second per-search limit.

Explicit settings: Threads 1, Hash 64 MiB, pinned `nn-1a298aa575a0.nnue`, LMR on,
classic time policy, MVV-LVA ordering, all other experimental options off,
pondering off, Syzygy off and diagnostics off. Unlike several historical
experiment profilers, this runner tests the accepted LMR-on baseline.

**All 120 searches passed exact parity.** Independent review reconstructed 720
raw iteration records and replayed 3,104 PV moves, plus final best/ponder moves.
Scores, nodes, depths, selective depths and complete PVs matched at every
iteration. All 60 baseline/candidate pairs and repeated searches agreed.
All 35 captured binary, source, tool, corpus and network identities matched
before and after the run. Hash occupancy also matched in the independent audit.

## Correctness and regression checks

- Full native `make test` and full ASan/UBSan `make test` passed in separate
  build directories. Forced-scalar tactical-generation and Search API tests passed.
- The new generator matched the established full legal generator on **8,018
  positions**, including 373 checked, 783 quiet-only, five terminal and 233
  null-search positions. Composed cases cover pins, legal/illegal en passant,
  castling, all promotions and a later capture after an early quiet witness.
  Deterministic legal walks and complete unwinds check incremental restoration.
- Search regressions exercise a quiet-only nonterminal leaf with nonzero
  stand-pat and quiet promotion mates in both colors. Existing terminal,
  rule-50, evasion, evaluation, policy and cancellation cases also pass.
- Native direct null-move, ProbCut and SEE-pruning tests passed, including
  speculative-state/cancellation and one/four-worker cases. The independent
  SMP UCI suite passed all 43 bounded lifecycle/search cases.
- The comparison tool passed 21 tests for exact signatures, legal PVs,
  complete matrices, input drift, failed/partial evidence, cleanup and limits.
- A separate bounded comparison used the owner's unchanged **39 FENs**, one
  worker, fresh state, the same explicit defaults and **20,000 nodes per search**.
  All 78 searches and 260 iteration records passed exact score/node/PV/depth
  and final-move parity. PVs and best/ponder moves replayed legally. The total
  was 1,560,000 nodes. This is a correctness check, not a 60-second solving audit;
  its elapsed times are not evidence because other correctness checks overlapped.

No sanitizer findings or reproducible search differences were observed. No
long campaign was started. Foreign runtime and TSan checks were not repeated
in this phase; the change adds no shared state or parallel synchronization.
