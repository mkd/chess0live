# Phase 4F.11 — affected-feature NNUE updates

Date: October 29, 2026. Parent: `1ca87d6`.
Decision: retain revision 2 for the bounded implementation-performance result;
no puzzle, Elo, rating, SMP-strength or release claim.

## What changed

The worker-local evaluator now discovers changed squares from the union of
twelve piece-bitboard XORs, once for both perspectives. It computes only
affected piece-square, threat and pawn-pair feature changes. Empty-board attack
geometry selects possibly affected sources; friendly relationships and feature
multiplicities are preserved. A king bucket/mirror change refreshes that
perspective. Both plans are prepared before accumulator mutation.

The original full enumeration/update remains the reference. Network format,
weights, calibration, arithmetic kernels, search policy and UCI options are
unchanged. This still compares successive evaluated boards; it does not add a
per-ply accumulator stack or refresh cache. Feature plans still allocate scratch
vectors. Further optimization requires separate evidence.

## Diagnosis and first candidate

The [initial protocol](../experiments/phase4f11.md) used all fifteen existing
exposed opening/middlegame/endgame roots with current defaults, one worker and
64 MiB Hash. Thirty measured searches followed fifteen warmups during a bounded
120-second sample. Of 2,980 active-search observations, 2,560 (85.91%) were in
NNUE, 1,879 (63.05%) in update and 778 (26.11%) in feature discovery. These
inclusive costs overlap; they are sampled stack observations, not exact CPU
cycle shares. Only 29 short-lived search threads received samples, so per-root
stack coverage is not established.

The initial sampler attempt was denied process inspection. Its failure evidence
was retained; a fresh authorized attempt completed. Independent replay checked
237 PV records / 948 legal PV plies, all 45 best/ponder pairs, settings, assets
and the measured sampling window. The engine PID was later absent, but this
sampler does not record a graceful engine exit code. The first analysis assumed
30 sampled threads and failed; the corrected analysis preserves the distinction
between completed searches and observed stacks.

Revision 1 passed exact correctness and search checks. Its three timing medians
were **1.048235, 1.044654 and 1.047207** parent/candidate seconds. However, warm
`gamephases-eg-02` had a pooled ratio **0.973793**, below the predeclared 0.98
guard. It did not pass acceptance; this is not proof of a general strength loss.
Its sources, binary, reports and failed decision remain preserved.

The separately declared [follow-up](../experiments/phase4f11-bitboard-delta.md)
replaced two 64-square scans with one shared bitboard change mask. No thresholds,
feature-selection rules or position-specific exceptions changed.

## Timing result

Both optimized binaries used identical `-O3 -DNDEBUG -g` C++26 flags. The owner
confirmed paused LM Studio inference, AC power and Automatic mode for short
comparisons. Each comparison used one active worker and two resident engines;
the build jobs stayed within the observed 18-CPU / 64-GiB daytime budget.

The parent-versus-parent control had median ratio **0.999329** across 60 pairs.
Individual short observations varied; this does not eliminate timing noise.
All three revision-2 runs used depth 6, all fifteen roots, cold and reused state,
three repetitions and alternating roles: **540 searches / 270 measured pairs**.

| Run | Median parent/candidate elapsed ratio |
| --- | ---: |
| 1 | 1.056821 |
| 2 | 1.049287 |
| 3 | 1.053932 |

All run medians exceed 1.03. The lowest pooled root/state median is **0.987471**
(warm `gamephases-eg-02`), above the 0.98 guard. The result is roughly **4.9–5.7%
greater equal-work execution rate** by the declared median metric, not uniform
acceleration across every position. The small cold/warm endgame-02 slowdowns
remain in the evidence.

Independent raw replay, including the 120-search control, checked **660 searches,
3,960 iterations and 17,072 legal PV plies**. Nodes, scores, PVs, best moves and
ponder match between parent/candidate, repeats and control. Asset checks and
recorded process exits pass. Timing ratios are recomputed from saved elapsed
observations; absolute start/end clock timestamps were not saved. Three early
audit schema/setup assumptions failed and were repaired; their records remain.
Revision 2 uses the corrected auditor unchanged.

This is exposed finite development data with engineering thresholds, not a
statistical confidence interval or independent held-out confirmation. No TSan
rerun, foreign-target execution, full equal-time puzzle comparison or strength
match is claimed in this phase.
