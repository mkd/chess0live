## Interpretation and next work

The pair-level interval assumes independent opening-pair observations. Related,
hand-selected opening families limit that assumption and generalization; this
suite is not a representative random sample or a calibrated rating pool. The
interval's half-width is about 16.976 percentage points. Confirmation review
required at least **86/128 points**, with a lower bound above 0.5; the candidate
scored 76. The upper bound also exceeds 0.5, so the predeclared result is
inconclusive rather than negative evidence or equivalence.

The observed lead is a reason to retain the research candidate, not to change
defaults or advertise an Elo gain. The raw summary's descriptive Elo transform
is not an absolute rating or proof of stronger play. Do not enlarge this finished
sample, change its criterion, or tune against these now-exposed openings while
calling them held out. The earlier unfavorable development smoke and this
larger favorable point estimate retain their separate scopes.

Keep **classic timing, LMR off, and MVV-LVA ordering** as defaults. The proposed
next step is **Phase 3A: establish a measured performance baseline** with those
settings, preserving correctness and the pinned NNUE oracle. No improvement
follows merely from measuring NPS or profiling code. Further LMR qualification
and separate SEE qualification are deferred; neither is completed or promoted
by this result. Any future strength campaign needs a fresh predeclared design
and resource window. No additional match or recurring schedule is launched by
this report; the full engine remains unreleased and unrated `0.1-dev`.
