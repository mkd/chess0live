## Time use

Rounded PGN comments reconstruct White's remaining clock as roughly 56.8s after
move 10, 28.3s after move 20 and 0.7s after move 30. Minor negative values later
are rounding artifacts, not evidence of a clock forfeit. With a full 120+1 clock
and 20ms overhead, classic nominal allocation is 4,549.375ms and its hard ceiling
18,197.5ms. A started iteration may run to that ceiling; a large discarded
iteration can consume the reserve. This is consistent with expensive opening
thinks, without proving a missed deadline in the historical game.

Optional `Time Policy=bounded` preserves soft allocation and stability signals,
but halves the ordinary clock-only hard multiplier to two. The example ceiling
becomes 9,098.75ms. Explicit work limits, movetime, infinite and ponder retain
their previous budgets. Classic remains default. Earlier stopping can also lose
useful depth, so no strength gain or guaranteed endgame reserve is claimed.
The fixed six-search replay completed with full game histories, reconstructed
remaining clocks, one worker, Hash 64, LMR on and deeper reductions off:

## Extra quiet reductions

`Deep Quiet Reductions` is false by default and requires accepted ordinary LMR.
Only eligible interior non-PV moves at depth >=5, after eight searched moves,
moving a knight/bishop/rook/queen with >7 pieces can receive total reduction two.
All inherited guards remain; promising probes verify directly at ordinary depth.
Policy changes invalidate cached bounds/history. Diagnostic counters aggregate
only after worker joins and report extra-tier attempts and completed outcomes.

A first activation assertion expected extra-tier re-search at startpos depth 7;
it failed because 54 extra reductions produced no alpha-raising extra-tier probe.
Six bounded diagnostic fixtures (about 7.9 seconds of search) likewise observed
extra reductions but no extra-tier re-search, while ordinary LMR verification
remained active. No policy parameter was changed. The corrected test interrupts
the first extra reduction at exactly 65,900 nodes, checks reversible state and
exercises the shared ordinary-LMR verification path. Extra-tier alpha-raising
verification was not observed in those fixtures and is not claimed as covered.
