# Phase 4D.7 — guarded tactical ProbCut

## Scope and identity

One independently authored, default-off hypothesis follows the
[fixed protocol](../experiments/phase4d7-probcut.md), committed as `563c040`
before implementation/searches. No other search policy or default changed.
The parent is source `8052b09906099fb169d1b9a1e114004fc68be473`, executable
SHA-256 `c1858bb92419e00bbe3846e2daa94dc4260effad6acea705a2455881c920c199`.
The frozen candidate SHA-256 is
`3d5856b8c023ca293d8e4ef66b93d0435a61b5b5840171da637ec20d7fa5b782`.
Both use the unchanged pinned `nn-1a298aa575a0.nnue` network.
Inputs are under `build/experiments/phase4d7-20261023/`; logs and raw evidence
are under `results/phase4d7-20261023/`.

`ProbCut` tests at most three ordinary captures at eligible interior scout
nodes, with SEE >=0, a quiescence screen and a legal-move search at child
depth `depth - 4`. Both must reach beta plus 200 internal score units outside
special bands. Check, history, material and draw-clock guards limit exposure.
The reduced legal search includes quiet defenses. The isolated probe mode
cannot consume/store TT bounds, recurse into pruning/LMR or learn ordering
state, but preserves genuine history and draw rules. A cutoff returns only
the original beta, without a probe PV or direct original-depth cache entry.
These rules are heuristic safeguards, not a proof of preserved strength.

## Correctness checks

Native, scalar, ASan+UBSan and TSan builds all passed direct search tests and
30 short UCI search/lifecycle cases each. The direct suite checks actual
two-stage activation, original-beta return, TT/PV exclusion, interrupted
second-stage entry, all policy boundaries and real mate/tablebase thresholds.
Both-color composed tactical fixtures exercise a quiet mating defense that
quiescence misses but the legal reduced search rejects; checking capture mates
remain ordinary mate evidence rather than ProbCut cutoffs. Negative-SEE
sacrifices remain in ordinary search even when excluded from optional probes.
An independent Python chess referee verifies the fixture chess facts.

Real repetition and rule-50 draws, complete probe-mode cache/learning isolation,
policy-toggle and diagnostics parity, exact one/four-worker node caps, expired
clocks, stop/ponder and post-cancellation reuse passed. Artificial interior
windows deliberately reach rare correctness branches; they are not strength
samples or tuned policy parameters. Native core/search/policy/time checks and
the existing 43-case SMP lifecycle suite passed. Scalar/ASan+UBSan also passed
ordinary search regressions. No sanitizer or race finding occurred.

Two independent code reviews found no production blocker. Review tightened
the counter invariant to `searches == qsearch_passes`, matching their paired
increments; this changed tests only. The profiler passed 31 engine-free checks
covering malformed/overflow diagnostics, legal output, hashfull/cache-only
parent differences, source drift, resource/deadline gates, interruption and
process cleanup. The README introduction, owner batch settings and protected
GUI bundles were preserved. No cross-platform execution is claimed.

## Single fixed development screen

The 60 searches in 36 serial engine sessions completed in **24.990 seconds**.
The predeclared six exposed roots, depth 6 and 500ms limits were not tuned or
repeated. Threads=1, Hash=64, Move Overhead=20, classic timing, MVV-LVA,
conservative LMR enabled; all unrelated experiments and Syzygy were disabled.
Diagnostics were enabled for accounting, so times are instrumented descriptive
measurements. No other development build or engine was running at launch.
The process snapshot and quiet-host declaration cannot prove constant OS load.

Candidate-off matched the frozen parent exactly in all 12 cold/warm pairs:
every completed iteration's nodes, score, PV, seldepth and hashfull; final
move/ponder; LMR, ordering and cache counters. The added option therefore
preserves the tested baseline when disabled.

| Depth-6 aggregate | ProbCut off | ProbCut on | Observed change |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Fresh-cache nodes | 939,871 | 732,586 | 22.05% fewer |
| Fresh-cache elapsed | 3.429s | 2.682s | 21.77% less |
| Reused-cache nodes | 135,081 | 131,052 | 2.98% fewer |
| Reused-cache elapsed | 0.423s | 0.430s | 1.62% more |

All 12 depth-6 final moves, scores and PVs matched, with no score/PV changes
at any common completed depth. Three reused-cache roots increased node work;
the benefit is not uniform. Much of the fresh-cache saving came from
`gamephases-mg-03` (419,285 to 249,918 nodes). Root-by-root differences, including
seldepth, cache counters and adverse timings, remain in `profile.json`.

In the six 500ms pairs (480ms effective budget), best moves all matched. Five
roots retained their completed depth; `opening-03` completed depth 6 instead
of 5, returning -26 rather than -16 UCI centipawns at that extra depth. Scores
and PVs matched at common depths. The endgame root's timed depth-8 work
fell from 225,462 to 120,465 cumulative nodes, but both still completed depth 8.
Total unfinished nodes increased from 377,831 to 396,575 across the timed set;
that work is included in totals rather than discarded from the report.

ProbCut completed 188 reduced probes/cutoffs in the fixed-depth comparisons.
Timed searches started 259 reduced probes, completed 258 cutoffs and interrupted
one. No reduced-probe rejection occurred in this screen; the independent quiet
defense regression covers that actual branch. All 29 profiled artifact hashes
remained unchanged. Counters and reduced work do not establish Elo or tactical
equivalence outside these finite tests.

Independent audits replayed all 359 PVs (1,626 legal plies), 60 best moves and
60 ponder moves, checked the complete session transcripts and recomputed the
aggregates. All 36 sessions recorded clean exits and all 179 common-depth
score/PV comparisons matched. Saved evidence is in `profile-audit.json`.
The audit also verified 62 protected files and the exact README introduction.

## Puzzle checkpoint and decision

The adapter's one-second smoke completed all 39 positions per build. Independent
saved-output audit verified all 78 legal final moves, ordered/unique rows,
valid scores, corpus identity and runner/adapter/binary/network pins. There
were no missing/error results or reported mates; all 39 best moves matched,
with 14 centipawn-score differences. `puzzles-smoke-audit.json` records every
difference. This checks the prepared workflow; it is not the full puzzle gate
or a best-move solve rate. The console session exited zero; the text outputs
alone do not independently record process exit codes.

The full matched puzzle comparison is prepared for the same 39 FENs in order,
60,000ms each, one worker and Hash=64. It uses the snapshotted unchanged owner
script via an isolated adapter. Both roles explicitly disable deeper reductions;
only the candidate enables ProbCut. The adapter preserves final exact-score
handling, one fresh engine process per position and cleanup. It does not change
the owner's editable script or implement the spreadsheet's any-depth points rule.

The full run is **not started**. It takes roughly 78 minutes plus startup and
requires the overnight window or a new one-run daytime exception under
[AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md). Earlier exceptions are consumed. No schedule or
long strength match was created. A frozen one-run wrapper passed syntax,
pin and resource preflight: one active worker, one resident engine, approximately
632MiB reserved against a detected 64GiB host and the 50% daytime policy.

Keep `ProbCut=false`. The fresh-cache saving and one extra timed ply justify
finishing the tactical comparison, but the weak reused-cache result and lack
of playing evidence prevent default promotion. After the finite puzzle audit,
record a qualification-or-parking decision without repeated tuning. A strength
claim still needs a separately frozen equal-resource match. The broader roadmap
then retains Phase 4E runtime/platform and network tooling work; this experiment
does not complete the original release acceptance gates.
